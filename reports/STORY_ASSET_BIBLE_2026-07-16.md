# SBZ AI Video Studio — Story Asset Bible

Fresh inventory date: **2026-07-16**

Use this document when asking ChatGPT to write stories that SBZ Studio can actually render. Names are copied from the current application catalog.

## Important capability rules

- **169 characters are selectable now:** 44 SBZ Originals + 125 Quaternius.
- The 43 SBZ mascot characters have **jaw-openness lip-sync**. They can carry normal dialogue, but do not claim detailed viseme faces or blinking.
- **Amy Humanoid** and all Quaternius characters are currently **body-performance characters**. Use short dialogue, narration, medium/wide shots and visible actions.
- **Quaternius Adventurer** is the strongest validated interactive body performer.
- Five Quaternius collection models are static: Casual, Formal, Medieval, SciFi and Soldier. Use them as crowd/background figures, not action leads.
- No currently integrated character is `FULL_FACIAL`. Do not request professional facial close-ups until a facial-ready model is added.

## 18. Copy-ready ChatGPT story request

```text
Write a professional YouTube cartoon story for SBZ AI Video Studio.

Language: 85% natural Hindi/Urdu in Roman script + 15% simple English.
Audience: children and family.
Duration: [insert duration].
Genre: [insert genre].
Moral/theme: [insert theme].

Use only these chosen characters from the SBZ Story Asset Bible:
- [exact character name]
- [exact character name]

Allowed environments:
- [Market / Kitchen / Garden / Forest / Indoor / Night / Desert / Snow / Storm / Mud / Wash / Stage]

Allowed props:
- Box, Ball, Book, Gift, Cup, Sign, Rock

Every spoken line must follow this exact format:
Character Name: (emotion; one supported action) natural dialogue.

Supported actions:
idle, look, listen, walk, run, approach, exit, wave, point, reach, pickup,
give, celebrate, jump, dance, nod, shake, hug, help, pull, punch, kick,
hit, fall, sit, stand, wash, slip, splash.

Requirements:
- Give every scene a clear location, time, lighting, weather, foreground,
  background depth and story prop.
- Make actions follow cause and effect; no random gestures.
- Use short lines for Quaternius/body-only characters and let body acting tell the story.
- Use normal dialogue for SBZ jaw-lip-sync mascots, but avoid claims of detailed facial acting.
- Include entrances, movement across space, prop interaction, reactions and exits.
- Use clean cuts for dialogue and motivated transitions only.
- Do not invent characters, props, facial controls or environments outside this list.
- Return a title, character cast, scene list, then the complete formatted script.
```
